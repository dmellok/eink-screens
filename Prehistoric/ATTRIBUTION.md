# Attribution — Prehistoric Life

Every plate is assembled from three open sources.

**Silhouettes** come from [PhyloPic](https://www.phylopic.org/). Each is credited below with its
artist, licence and image id. Where PhyloPic had no image for the species or genus, a
higher-taxon silhouette was used and the matched rank is shown *in italics* — those plates
illustrate the group, not the individual species.

**Occurrence, age, taxonomy and ecology data** come from the
[Paleobiology Database](https://paleobiodb.org/) (PBDB), retrieved 2026-08-13. PBDB data is
contributed by researchers worldwide and released under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

**Coastlines** are [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 110m land (public domain),
drawn on an Equal Earth projection. Occurrence dots use modern coordinates, not palaeocoordinates.

**Body size** figures are typical adult literature estimates, hand-compiled; they are not from
PBDB and are labelled as estimates on the plate.

**Type** is [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) (SIL Open Font License 1.1) with binomials set in Times.
